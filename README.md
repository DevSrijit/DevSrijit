### Srijit Ghosh

Design engineer in Kolkata. I build whole products alone, from the design system to the servers.

I am the cofounder and CTO of [CommSync](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6810334130), an AI inbox for email and SMS. I designed and built all of it: the Next.js web app, the iPhone app, the Bun backend and the infrastructure. I also build AI agents and internal tools for a US marketing agency.
